        Hardware architecture

        Proposed real implementation. This prototype simulates every sensor value in the browser. No ESP32, ultrasonic sensor or load cell is connected.

        Per bin: one ESP32. Per compartment: one ultrasonic sensor (such as HC-SR04) and one load cell with an HX711 amplifier.

        ESP32→Ultrasonic sensor→Wet waste fill level
        ESP32→Load cell + HX711→Wet waste weight
        ESP32→Ultrasonic sensor→Dry waste fill level
        ESP32→Load cell + HX711→Dry waste weight
        ESP32→Cloud / API→SMARTCLEAN AI→Prediction→Priority→Route→Operator

        Weight alone does not give the exact fill level, because waste density varies. SMARTCLEAN AI combines ultrasonic fill level, load-cell weight and historical growth rate. Priority = 0.35 × fill + 0.35 × weight + 0.30 × predicted fill. A compartment is flagged when fill or weight reaches 85%, or predicted fill reaches 90%.
